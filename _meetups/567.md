description: Last year, 61 people died in interactions with the police or in incarceration facilities across the country. Historically, we lacked means for systematically tracking even basic details of the circumstances around these deaths, including rudimentary demographic information (who was killed?), geographic (where were they killed?), as well as investigative outcomes (were any officials held responsible?), and journalistic coverage (has there been any reporting about this)? With a team of researchers spanning Carleton, McGill, Queens, UofT, and UBC, we are developing the [Tracking (In)Justice project](https://trackinginjustice.ca/) to systematically and transparently catalogue the circumstances around these deaths, drawing on a mix of sources from government releases, news reporting, and meticulous case-by-case fact checking, tracking these critical details from 2000 onwards.
