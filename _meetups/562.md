description: "Governments in Canada and abroad are moving to protect children online: banning kids from social media, mandating safer defaults, restricting what platforms can recommend to young users. Much of it depends on first establishing how old users are. In this talk, Sara Grimes looks at the infrastructure these proposals routinely require, including AI-driven facial estimation and behavioural inference, applied to everyone, collecting more data from children in the name of protecting them. She'll ask what the evidence says about whether these technologies work and what their spreading implementation means for children’s privacy and other rights."
