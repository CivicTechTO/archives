description: "Understanding what communities need from housing and transit, and deploying evidence-based responses to those needs is hard. Who's living in housing around transit infrastructure? What amenities are in their neighbourhood, and what's missing? What housing stock exists? How can it be developed further to serve the local community effectively? And of course, how does walkability fit into this whole picture?\r

  \r

  To equip community leaders with tools to answer these questions and more, the [Canadian Urban Institute (CUI)](https://canurb.org/) developed Measuring Main Streets. It's a digital platform bringing together unique neighbourhood-level data on demographics, housing development potential, and community amenities in and around transit station areas.\r

  \r

  We'll demo the platform's tools and show how they go beyond traditional transit-oriented development, which tends to focus on density, to make the case for what we call transit-oriented complete communities: places where transit investment also delivers the amenities and housing mix people actually need."
