description: |-
  What does it take to build a less lonely city? Can we scale human connection the same way we scale a tech startup?

  Happy Town began as a grassroots experiment in creating spaces where strangers could become friends. Through recurring walks, shared meals and community gatherings, it has grown into an evolving network hosts creating opportunities for more satisfying connections across Toronto.

  But growing a community comes with distinct challenges not faced by companies or start ups.

  What happens when growth creates pressure on the very relationships we’re trying to nurture? What if the most sustainable way to scale community isn’t to build a better product, but to build a movement?

  Drawing on the experience of building Happy Town and its evolution into Less Lonely Winter, a collective effort to bring 25+ free community events per week to Toronto throughout winter, this talk explores our approach to community building. One rooted in the idea that nobody owns community; and the belief that connection is grown, not found.
