description: Farhan Samir is a Natural Sciences and Engineering Research Council (NSERC) Postdoctoral Fellow in the Computer Science department at the University of Toronto. He earned his PhD at the University of British Columbia, where his work was supported by an NSERC Doctoral Fellowship, a UBC Public Scholars Initiative award, and the 2024-25 UBC Department of Statistics Award in Data Science. He was formerly a researcher at NVIDIA, Amazon Science, and the Allen Institute for AI.
