title: Carrie Mitchell
description: Carrie Mitchell is an Associate Professor in the School of Planning at the University of Waterloo, where she studies urban climate resilience, community-based planning, and, more recently, road safety governance. She is the founder of CIVIC ([OurCIVICFutures.ca](https://ourcivicfutures.ca/)), an urban living lab funded by Waterloo’s Global Futures Fund that is deploying privacy-by-design traffic and noise sensors with residents and community partners. Before joining Waterloo, she spent five years at Canada’s International Development Research Centre (IDRC) managing urban environment and climate research across Asia. She lives in London, Ontario.
