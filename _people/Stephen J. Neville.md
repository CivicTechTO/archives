title: Stephen J. Neville
description: Stephen J. Neville is a media and surveillance scholar based in Guelph, Ontario. Currently, he is a Fellow at the Infoscape Research Lab at Toronto Metropolitan University. He was the principal investigator for “Losing your voice to AI”, funded by the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (2025-2026), which mapped the emerging industry of health-related machine listening to address problems of data privacy, consent, and transparency. With Greg Elmer, he is the coauthor of [The Politics of Media Scarcity](https://www.routledge.com/The-Politics-of-Media-Scarcity/Elmer-Neville/p/book/9781032504698) (Routledge 2024) which foregrounds marginalized histories and storytelling as a challenge to dominant narratives of abundance and digital divides in media studies.
