description: |-
  Steve Till-Rogers is the Director of Digital Strategy and Chief Information Officer with the Toronto Public Library. TPL is Canada’s largest public library with 100 branches located throughout Toronto. Steve leads delivery of TPL’s Digital Strategy executing IT service modernization, innovation and enhancing digital access.

  Prior to joining Toronto Public Library, Steve was the Director of Technology Services with Edmonton Public Library. During his time at EPL, Steve deployed new technologies and digital experiences within the library including The Wall, at the Stanley A. Milner Library.

  Previously named as a Digital Game Changer by HP Enterprise, Steve is recognized for his expertise in digital infrastructure, integrated systems, and leveraging technology to enhance library services and patron experiences.
